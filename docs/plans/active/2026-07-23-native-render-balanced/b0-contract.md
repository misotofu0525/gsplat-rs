# B0 Balanced Contract

## Hypothesis

Resident performance trade-offs can be evaluated independently without silently
reducing source count, SH degree or render resolution.

## Required decision record

- Name the fidelity fields, image/error gate, camera traces and receipts before
  an approximation is implemented.
- Keep Exact and Balanced plans disjoint and make any enablement opt-in until
  later endpoint qualification.
- Declare each subsequent B1--B3 experiment as Accepted, Rejected or Deferred
  from its own evidence; no fixed FPS or competitor percentage is a completion
  gate.

No implementation decision is made by this contract alone.
