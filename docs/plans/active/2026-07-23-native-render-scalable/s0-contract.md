# S0 Scalable Contract

## Hypothesis

Scenes beyond resident budgets need independently valid hierarchical coverage,
not a smaller arbitrary subset of source splats.

## Required decision record

- Identify the authored/proxy asset approach and its parent/child coverage
  invariant.
- Separate compressed, decoded and GPU byte budgets from frame latency and
  quality receipts.
- Require metadata-first admission: opening a large scene must not construct a
  full `SceneBuffers` allocation before the contract selects a bounded path.

S0 is planning and research only; it does not claim a streamed product runtime.
